Nusa Data is an independent reading of Indonesia's official statistics. Every figure comes from the World Bank's World Development Indicators, pulled on 26 November 2026; each chart names the series it draws and the year of its latest value.

Values are reproduced as published, at the publisher's own precision. Gaps in a series are years the World Bank does not report, and the charts break the line there rather than interpolate.
